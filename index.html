What We Offer

Preschool Programs in Aurangabad

From playgroup to Sr KG — a nurturing journey tailored to every age at our Aurangabad preschool.

🧒

Playgroup in Aurangabad (Ages 2–3)

Our playgroup in Aurangabad introduces toddlers to a world of sensory play, rhymes, and social interaction. A gentle first step into structured learning through exploration and fun.

🎨

Nursery School in Aurangabad (Ages 3–4)

The nursery school program at our Aurangabad centre builds early literacy, numeracy, and creative expression. Children develop confidence through hands-on activities and guided play.

📖

Jr KG School in Aurangabad (Ages 4–5)

Our Jr KG in Aurangabad focuses on pre-reading, math concepts, and school readiness. Children build vocabulary, problem-solving skills, and a love for structured learning.

🚀

Sr KG School in Aurangabad (Ages 5–6)

The Sr KG program in Aurangabad prepares children for primary school with reading fluency, advanced numeracy, and critical thinking. A strong foundation for lifelong learning.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What age does TinyTots accept?

TinyTots Kindergarten accepts children from 2 years old. Our playgroup in Aurangabad (Chh Sambhajinagar) offers a nurturing, play-based environment for young learners. We have programs for Playgroup (2–3 yrs), Nursery (3–4 yrs), Jr KG (4–5 yrs), and Sr KG (5–6 yrs).

Where is TinyTots Kindergarten located?

TinyTots Kindergarten is located on Adalat Road, behind the Royal Enfield Showroom, in Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar), Maharashtra 431005. We are easily accessible from all parts of the city.

What is the fee structure at TinyTots Kindergarten?

Our preschool fees in Aurangabad are affordable and vary by program (Playgroup, Nursery, Jr KG, Sr KG). Please contact us at +91 9225560007 or visit our campus on Adalat Road for detailed fee information and available discounts.

Does TinyTots offer Jr KG and Sr KG programs?

Yes! TinyTots is a full Jr KG and Sr KG school in Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar) offering Junior KG (4–5 yrs) and Senior KG (5–6 yrs) programs. Our curriculum focuses on reading, numbers, creative arts, and school readiness to prepare children for primary education.
