How to Choose the Right Preschool in Aurangabad: A Parent's Checklist

A simple checklist to take along on your school visits — so you can choose a place where your child feels happy, safe and curious.

Photo by Gautam Arora on Unsplash

November 2, 2026

Choosing a preschool is one of the first big decisions you make for your child, and in a growing city like Aurangabad there are many options to consider. Every school will tell you it is caring and child-friendly, so how do you see the difference for yourself? Our teachers have put together a simple checklist to take along when you visit preschools — so that you can look beyond the brochure and choose the place where your child will feel happy, safe and curious.

1. Start with Your Child, Not the School

Before you visit anywhere, spend a few minutes thinking about your own child. Is she shy with new people or does she run straight to other children? Does he still nap in the afternoon? Does your family speak Marathi, Hindi or English at home? A good preschool is not the one with the most features — it is the one that suits your child's age, temperament and daily rhythm.

Also think about the practical side: the timings you need, who will do drop-off and pick-up, and how far you are comfortable travelling every morning. A shorter, calmer journey often makes for a happier start to the day.

2. Look for Learning Through Play

Young children learn best by doing — building, pouring, singing, pretending and talking. India's National Education Policy 2020 describes early childhood education as play-based and activity-based, and that is a helpful yardstick when you visit. Look around the classroom and ask:

  • Are there blocks, puzzles, picture books, art materials and pretend-play corners within children's reach?
  • Is children's own artwork on the walls, or only printed charts?
  • Do two- and three-year-olds spend most of their time playing and exploring, rather than sitting with worksheets?
  • Is there time for music, stories and movement every day?

Be a little cautious of a school that promises a toddler will be writing full sentences early. At this age, curiosity, language and confidence matter far more than speed.

3. Watch How Teachers Talk to Children

The teachers are the heart of any preschool. If you can, visit while classes are running and simply watch for a few minutes. Do teachers kneel down to the child's eye level? Do they speak gently and patiently, even when a child is upset? Do children go to them easily for help?

It is also fair to ask how many children are in each class, how many adults are with them, and how long the teachers have been with the school. A settled, caring team gives little ones the security they need to try new things.

4. Check Safety and Cleanliness Carefully

This is the part of the visit where you should feel free to ask every question on your mind. Things to notice:

  • Entry and pick-up: Who is allowed to collect a child, and how does the school check?
  • Classrooms and play areas: Covered sockets, safe furniture without sharp edges, soft flooring where children climb and run.
  • Toilets and hand-washing: Child-sized, clean and supervised, with soap and water at hand.
  • Drinking water and food: Clean drinking water, and clear rules about tiffin and food allergies.
  • Health: What happens if a child feels unwell during the day, and how quickly parents are informed.

5. Ask About Daily Routine and Settling In

Ask the school to walk you through a typical day — arrival, circle time, play, snack, outdoor time and home time. Young children feel secure when they know what comes next.

Equally important is how the school helps new children settle. Many little ones cry for the first few days, and that is completely normal. Ask whether parents can stay for a short while at first, whether timings are shorter in the first week, and how teachers comfort a child who misses home.

6. Understand Communication and Age Rules

Find out how teachers will keep you informed — a daily diary, parent–teacher meetings, phone calls or messages — and how you can raise a concern. A school that welcomes questions now will usually be easy to talk to later.

It is also worth asking about age cut-offs. The Maharashtra government has recently announced revised minimum ages for Nursery, Jr KG, Sr KG and Class 1, to be introduced in phases from the 2027–28 academic year. As detailed guidelines are still being issued, ask the school which age rule applies to your child's year of admission and how it fits with their later move to Class 1. If you are still deciding which class suits your child, our guide to Playgroup, Nursery, Jr KG and Sr KG may help.

7. Trust What You and Your Child Feel

After all the questions, notice how the place feels. Did the children look busy and cheerful? Did your own child want to stay and play a little longer? Did you leave feeling reassured? A parent's instinct, together with a child's reaction, often tells you more than any list.

Visiting two or three preschools in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar before you decide is a good idea — it gives you something to compare and helps you feel confident in your choice.

Come and See for Yourself

Choosing a preschool does not need to feel overwhelming. With a clear checklist and an open conversation with the teachers, you will know when you have found the right place.
