Instagram connection

How to connect (one-time, about 10 minutes)
  1. In the Instagram app: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account (choose Business or Creator). Skip this if it already is one.
  2. On a computer, go to developers.facebook.com/apps and log in with Facebook. Click Create app, choose the Business type, and name it "TinyTots Scheduler".
  3. In the app, add the Instagram product and open API setup with Instagram login.
  4. Under Generate access tokens, click Add account, log in as @tinytotskg, then click Generate token and copy it.
  5. Paste the token below and press Connect. The scheduler renews it automatically.
